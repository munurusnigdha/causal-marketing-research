# Causal Evaluation of Email Marketing Effectiveness

## Research Objective
Evaluate whether randomized email marketing campaigns increase customer conversions and revenue.

## Dataset
Hillstrom Email Marketing Experiment — 64,000 customers assigned to men's email, women's email, or no email.

## Methods
- Python and pandas for data analysis
- Randomized controlled experiment
- A/B testing and hypothesis testing
- Confidence intervals for treatment effects
- Revenue analysis

## Key Findings
- Men's email increased conversion by 0.681 percentage points and revenue by approximately $0.77 per customer.
- Women's email increased conversion by 0.311 percentage points and revenue by approximately $0.42 per customer.
- Both campaigns showed statistically significant conversion increases relative to the control group.

## Research Limitations
This is a secondary analysis of an existing randomized experiment. Campaign profitability was not evaluated because marketing cost and product margin information were not included.

## Tools
Python, pandas, NumPy, SciPy, statsmodels, Matplotlib, Google Colab
